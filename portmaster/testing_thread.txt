Instructions to install the Testing Zip:

or
run via ssh: harbourmaster install "zip url from discord"

Game Information
Title: Colin McRae Rally 2.0
URL: https://github.com/pablocpas/OpenCMR2

Instructions:
Copy the folders Game, FrontEnd, Sounds, CountrySpecific and SetupRep from your own copy of Colin McRae Rally 2.0 for PC to ports/opencmr2/gamedata/.

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|DPAD| Move in menus, steer |
|Left Stick| Steer |
|A| Confirm / Handbrake |
|B| Back / Look back |
|Y| Change camera |
|L1| Gear down |
|R1| Gear up |
|L2| Brake |
|R2| Accelerate |
|Start| Pause and resume |
|Start + Select| Quit the game |
